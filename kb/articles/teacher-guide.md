title: Teachers: pointing a child to help
audience: adult
status: Draft. Needs review by a child-protection specialist.
---
You do not have to decide what happens next. Your job is to listen and point the child to help.

# What to say
- "Thank you for telling me. You did the right thing."
- "You can report it on Habari, and trained people will help you."
- "If you are in danger now, call 116. It is free."

# What to do
- Show the child how to report on Habari, or let them use a school device privately.
- If the child is in danger now, tell the school's safeguarding focal person.
- If the person hurting the child works at the school, Habari sends the report outside the school automatically.
