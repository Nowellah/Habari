---
title: Officer procedure: an adult in charge is the alleged person
audience: officer
status: Draft. The protocol must be agreed with a child-protection specialist before it is called a rule.
---
When the alleged person is a parent, guardian, teacher, coach or other adult in charge:

- Habari routes the case outside the school and home only: Sauti 116, an outside counsellor, Police CFPU and UCC.
- The school focal person and the family are not told.
- The accused is never notified through Habari. Any right to respond goes through the proper authority.
- Treat the case as severity 3 at least, and acknowledge it within the SLA.
