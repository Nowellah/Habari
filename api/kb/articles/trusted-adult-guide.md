---
title: Parents and carers: when a child tells you something
audience: adult
status: Draft. Needs review by a child-protection specialist.
---
How you react in the first minutes decides whether the child keeps talking to you.

# Do
- Stay calm and thank them for telling you.
- Tell them it is not their fault.
- Listen first. Ask simple questions like "what happened next?"
- Keep the messages. Do not delete anything.
- Get help together: Habari, or call **116**.

# Do not
- Do not shout, punish or take the phone away as a punishment.
- Do not contact the person who hurt them yourself.
- Do not share the child's story with other people.
