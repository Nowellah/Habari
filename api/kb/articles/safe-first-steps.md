---
title: What to do first if something bad happens online
audience: child
status: Draft. Needs review by a child-protection specialist.
---
You are not in trouble, and it is not your fault.

# Do these things now
- Stop replying to the person.
- Do not pay anything, not money, not airtime, not pictures.
- Do not delete the messages. They help the people who help you.
- Save the username or phone number of the person.
- Tell an adult you trust, or tell Habari.

# If you are in danger
Call **116**. It is free, and someone will listen.
