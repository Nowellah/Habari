If someone asks you to send pictures, or says they will share your pictures unless you send money, airtime or more pictures, this is not your fault. It is a crime, and they are the one doing wrong.

# What to do
- Do not send anything more and do not pay. Paying usually makes them ask for more.
- Stop replying, but do not delete the chat.
- Save their username, number and the link to their profile.
- Report it with Habari. A helper will work with the police and UCC to stop them.

# Remember
Many children have been through this and got help. You can too.
